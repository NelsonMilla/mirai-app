Section: the mission block on the Residency 2027 homepage (`components/residency/Program.tsx`), right after the JAPAN-behind-Fuji opening. The copy is fixed: the eyebrow "Who we are", the statement "10,000 cures" and four paragraphs (community · 10,000 rare diseases and the goal · Japan's ASRM path · Kobe University and the network).

## Five moves to try

**1. Read it into light.** *(NOT A HOTEL 08, monopo 09b, Lila 03)*
- **On arrival:** the 未来 mark and "Who we are" sit top-left. Below, the whole block is one continuous left-aligned text at a single display size (about 48–56px desktop, about 28px phone, about 40px for /jp), all in dim slate. "10,000 cures" is its first line, the same size and no bigger.
- **What carries the emotion:** reading. As the visitor scrolls, each line warms from slate to cream at reading height (scroll-linked, opacity only; fully lit without JS or with reduced motion). When "10,000 cures" lights, it turns dawn gold, the only accent, and the same gold returns on "10,000 cures" inside paragraph 2. There is no glow and no horizon: the type itself is the light, so this is not s-program's one-off effect behind the number.
- **The paragraphs:** all four are the display text, separated by a blank line. They become the show instead of a caption block. Using one size keeps it from being dizzying, and the reading pace keeps it from being boring.

**2. Answer with the people.** *(Retro 01, monopo 09, Beyond the Diagnosis 04)*
- **On arrival:** "10,000 cures" is set at sentence scale (about 64–80px, left, no glow), with paragraph 1 ("…a community for frontier biotech…") beside it as the lead. Directly below is one wide, graded, full-bleed real group photograph (`people-group-laughing-01` or `meal-long-table-01`) with its manifest caption in mono.
- **Variant:** a monopo-style hang of 5–7 small graded prints (long-table dinner, stage panel, lab visit, Kyoto day, station platform) placed in the gaps around the statement, settling into place once.
- **What carries the emotion:** faces. "Who we are" is answered by *who*, not by a number.
- **The paragraphs:** 2–4 sit under the photo in one quiet 17–18px column. Never put type on faces, never lead with a wide selfie or put two selfies next to each other, and don't use `lab-kobe-university-01`.

**3. Problem, then promise.** *(Our World in Data 06, Isomorphic 02, Retro 01)*
- **On arrival:** two lines of identical size and weight, stacked. The first, in slate, is paragraph 2's opening sentence (more than 10,000 rare diseases). The second, in cream with gold numerals, is the statement "10,000 cures". It is the same number with two meanings.
- **What carries the emotion:** the turn from problem to promise. The eye does the comparison, quietly. The second line arrives once, when the first reaches reading height (opacity, about 1.2s). There is no counter and no progress.
- **The paragraphs:** the rest of paragraph 2 follows as the lead, then paragraph 1, then 3–4 in a smaller column. Every word is verbatim copy, but the order changes (part of paragraph 2 moves above the statement), so this needs the owner's OK on order.

**4. Ten thousand you can see.** *(Our World in Data 06, Beyond the Diagnosis 04)*
- **On arrival:** "10,000 cures" at text scale above a quiet square field of 10,000 identical marks (100 × 100) in faint slate on night, set beside paragraph 2. The marks stand for the rare diseases the paragraph names: all alike, none lit.
- **What carries the emotion:** scale felt as area, and the stillness of 10,000 equal marks.
- **The paragraphs:** paragraph 1 as the lead, paragraph 2 next to the field, 3–4 below.
- **Rules:**
  - Static: no per-mark animation, no filling and no lit marks, since those would read as cures achieved or a progress bar.
  - Marks big and sparse enough not to shimmer (≥4px dots at ≥10px pitch).
  - At most a tiny mono label saying what one mark is, and only if the truth reviewer accepts it as a restatement of the copy.
- **Trade-off:** the riskiest move on truth and craft, and the strongest "see it" moment.

**5. The long view, set in Japan.** *(Long Now 07/07b, Shishi-Iwa House 11, Igloo 12)*
- **On arrival:** a narrow essay column on night: "10,000 cures" at a modest display size, then paragraphs 1–2 in 20–22px Switzer with generous leading.
- **The photo band:** between paragraph 2 (why) and paragraph 3 (Japan's ASRM path), a full-bleed graded Japan band from our own photos (`japan-kobe-harbor-01`, `japan-forest-bridge-01` or `japan-ropeway-01`) with its mono caption. It works the way Long Now cuts to the mountain, and the band marks the move from the problem to the place. One small vermilion hanko or 未来 glyph marks the turn, like Long Now's round glyph.
- **What carries the emotion:** the gravity of a long project, and arriving in Japan.
- **The paragraphs:** 3–4 (ASRM, Kobe University) come after the band.
- **Risk:** this is closest to the calm try the owner called boring. It only works if the band is big and the photo strong. Check the repetition budget against the Kobe section before using the harbour photo.

Moves 1 and 2 combine naturally: monopo runs both on one page, with the photo cluster around the title and the manifesto lighting below it.

## Diagnosis

All six tries treat "10,000 cures" as a graphic to be staged: first huge, then calmer, then lit by a dawn glow. Each time the four paragraphs are left as a caption block underneath. The references that land do the opposite and give the emotion to something the number belongs to:

- **People next to the claim:** Retro, monopo and Beyond the Diagnosis.
- **Reading as the event:** NOT A HOTEL, monopo and Lila.
- **The claim at sentence size, with restraint carrying the conviction:** Isomorphic and Retro.
- **Ten thousand tied to a mountain and a lifetime:** Long Now.

Our number has nothing attached: no faces, no place and no reading gesture. So "huge" was loud, "calm" was empty, and "dawn" is a decoration. It is a centred stat over a glow, a template tell, and it repeats the opening's dawn a second time, so it reads as an echo rather than an answer. The owner's own references (NOT A HOTEL, monopo) are about people and reading pace, not light effects. The next round should:

- keep the statement at text scale;
- spend the emotion on our real people or on the act of reading;
- let the four paragraphs be part of the show rather than footnotes.
