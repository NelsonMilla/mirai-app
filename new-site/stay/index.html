Getting there

Port Island is easy to reach.

From Kansai Airport (KIX)

The Bay Shuttle ferry crosses to Kobe Airport in about 30 minutes. From Kobe Airport the Port Liner runs straight through Port Island to Sannomiya.

From Osaka or Kyoto

Trains to Sannomiya take about 20 minutes from Osaka and an hour from Kyoto. Change to the Port Liner at Sannomiya.
