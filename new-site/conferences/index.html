Why Japan

A year to market, not a decade.

Under the Act on the Safety of Regenerative Medicine1, a cell or gene therapy can be sold on a self-pay basis once it has Phase 1 safety data, in six to twelve months. For small molecules, biologics and devices, Conditional Early Approval2 does the same nationally, around four years ahead of the FDA. Japan designed both routes deliberately, and founders arrive to learn them.
