Notes
- Flickr-sourced files are capped at 1024px (larger sizes not public).
  Fine under a dark/grain overlay treatment; avoid full-viewport hero use.
  harborland-rink.jpg is 1600px (Wikimedia original) — safe for wide use.
- lanterns.jpg, izakaya-counter.jpg, izakaya-alley.jpg were shot in
  Osaka/Tokyo, not Kobe — caption honestly (izakaya culture is generic;
  don't pin them to a named Kobe location).
- Rejected during curation: Ginzan Onsen night shots (gorgeous but
  Yamagata, not Arima — dishonest caption risk), a lantern shot with a
  Coca-Cola logo (trademark), several dark/noisy harbor frames.
