# Brief

## Who are we?

We are Mirai Tech City. We are a community focused on Frontier Biotech and how to deploy it fast and safe. Right now our goal is to get to "10,000 cures" which means utilizing ASRM to deploy assets quickly for first-in-human studies. We have collaboration with Kobe University and a very extensive network in Japanese Biotech.

Our past program did really well: (Cohort 0) We recieved 7 companies of which 5 are establishing a permanent presence in Japan, some of them were picked up by Antler, others got in-kind support by GlobalDeal, and all of them made new partnerships in Japan thanks to us.

You can find in the current codebase more results.

## Why is the landing page changing?

We are preparing to launch a new program: Our Residency for 2026. For this program we are going to spend a month (March) with startups in Mount Fuji, where they will be working on their companies and on their landing plan in Japan. Startups recieve:

- Workshops on how to submit an ASRM research proposal, and how ASRM works in Japan.
- Polish and prepare their unit economics and financials to be investable.
- Map out the connections that they want from Japan, and then in the last week we arrange meetings for them with such stakeholders.
- Learn how to pitch their company (Pitch fight club), and startup basics.

The program is intended for early stage startups that are working directly with RegenMed or in a supportive field to RegenMed.

Last week we move from Fuji (deep focus work) into KBIC in Kobe (Meetings inside of the cluster with different stakeholders).

We finish the program with a big demo day with investors including Tim Draper.
